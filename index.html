Gamified learning ecosystemWeb platform · UX / UI · Gamification

Humanizing the digital experience ✱

Spyro Challenge helps developers and professionals build UX knowledge through interactive modules, challenges and a card game.

01 The challenge

CRM systems are too complex, and nobody enjoys learning them.

In an increasingly digital world, CRM systems have become essential tools for managing business operations. Yet many of these platforms fail to meet user expectations: clunky interfaces, steep learning curves and limited personalization create friction, frustration and, ultimately, abandonment.

The challenge was to design a more human-centered experience that is intuitive, engaging and easy to integrate into daily workflows.

Project
Spyro Challenge
Type
Web platform, UX/UI design
Focus
Gamified learning & CRM adoption
Audience
Developers & professionals
02 Insights

Interview results, in numbers.

0188%

of users abandon a software platform if it feels too complicated or unintuitive.

0250%

of industrial users say poorly designed software causes frustration and disinterest, directly affecting productivity.

03Hands-on

Users in professional environments respond better to gamified, scenario-based learning than to static manuals or theory.

03 Needs

Meet Luis, the user behind the numbers.

“I don’t have time to fight with software. I need tools that work with me, not against me.”

Needs

Luis is a highly capable backend developer who writes clean, scalable code. His daily work revolves around logic, databases and system architecture. When it comes to UX/UI, though, he often feels out of his depth. He is frequently asked to implement design decisions without fully understanding the user experience behind them.

04 Problem & solution

From a manual to a game worth playing.

Problem

CRM systems in industrial and technical environments are often complex, unintuitive and not designed with users in mind. This leads to frustration, high abandonment rates, long onboarding processes and poor collaboration between designers and developers. Many developers are expected to implement interfaces without understanding UX principles, causing misalignment, inefficiencies and low user satisfaction.

Solution

Spyro Challenge is a gamified learning ecosystem designed to humanize the digital experience. It equips developers and professionals with accessible UX knowledge through interactive modules, challenges and a physical card game. By combining gamification, personalization and interactivity, it simplifies UX concepts, boosts CRM adoption, strengthens cross-team collaboration and makes learning engaging, practical and continuous.

Three ingredients

  • Gamification
  • Personalization
  • Interactivity

The outcome

UX concepts become simple, CRM adoption grows, cross-team collaboration gets stronger and learning stays engaging, practical and continuous.
